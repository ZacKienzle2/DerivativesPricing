The digital constructions are similar to feedback shift register random number generators, while lattice rules are similar to congruential generators. One useful way to think of QMC is that we are utilising a small random number generator in its entirety. The tools used to develop and analyse low-discrepancy methods are very different from those used in ordinary Monte Carlo, as they draw on number theory and abstract algebra rather than probability and statistics.
